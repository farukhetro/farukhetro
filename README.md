## 👋 About Me

I'm driven by curiosity and enjoy exploring new ideas, challenging assumptions, and solving real-world problems.

I enjoy working across **software, AI, products, systems, and business** rather than staying inside a single technology or discipline.

My approach is based on:

```text
🧠 First-Principles Thinking
       +
🛠️ Practical Engineering
       +
🚀 Fast Experimentation
       +
📈 Continuous Learning
       =
💡 Useful Products
```

I like building things that are **useful, scalable, and capable of creating measurable impact**.

Whether it's an open-source project, an AI tool, an automation system, or a product idea, I care about taking something from **idea → implementation → real-world use**.

### 🔭 Currently

* 🚀 Building **products that solve real-world problems**
* 🤖 Exploring **AI, automation, and intelligent systems**
* 🧠 Learning **system thinking, product design, and entrepreneurship**
* 🌱 Exploring **emerging technologies and new business models**
* 👯 Open to collaborating on **open-source and meaningful products**
* 💡 Interested in ideas that combine **technology + business + impact**
